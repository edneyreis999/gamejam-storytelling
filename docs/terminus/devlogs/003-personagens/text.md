# DevLog #3 — Getting to know the people of Terminus

The quests gave us a reason for people to meet again later in the game. Now we've been working on who those people actually are, and this part has been fun to think through.

We've put together character sheets for the cast of Terminus, our visual novel with RPG elements. There are 40 so far, including supporting characters and a reserve of ideas we might use later. That number sounds a bit scary for a jam. Some of those characters are there to give us options while writing; they aren't all promised a place in the finished game.

Most of the work has been about figuring out what each person wants and how that comes out in a conversation. How do they ask for help? What happens when someone disagrees with them? We're trying out short lines in similar situations to hear where their voices differ.

One character we're working on is an innkeeper who offers you a seat before asking why you've come. She wants people to feel welcome, but she takes on too much and gets frustrated when nobody helps. We like imagining her trying to have a serious conversation while there's still dinner to make. Those small details make us want to write scenes with her.

The character sheets are still drafts. Names and relationships may change, and the dialogue exercises aren't finished game scenes. But it's getting easier to picture the people behind the quest choices, which makes us more curious about how those choices will feel.

There's still a lot of writing between these sheets and a playable game. For now, we're looking forward to putting a few of these people in the same scene and seeing whether we can tell who's speaking without checking the name.
