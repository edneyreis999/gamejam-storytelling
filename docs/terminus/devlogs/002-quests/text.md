# DevLog #2 — Our quest system finally clicked. Now we have to build it.

We decided to make a visual novel with RPG elements for the jam, and we’ve figured out how our quests will work. It’s exciting to reach the point where the ideas start fitting together and you can actually picture someone playing.

The idea is that choices carry forward through the campaign. You’ll get to know different guilds, choose one to join, and build alliances along the way. Optional quests will let you recruit companions, but bringing one person into your group means giving up the chance to recruit someone else.

The part that got us really excited was figuring out how those people come back later. Someone you didn’t recruit might end up facing you in the tournament. A guild you chose not to support will be there too. We want those encounters to mean something because of what happened in earlier quests. You might even find yourself competing against someone you helped.

Seeing that structure come together made us want to play our own game. That’s probably been the best feeling so far.

Then we looked at how much we still have to do.

We’ve settled on the quest structure, but we still need to write the scenes, connect the choices to their consequences, and test the different paths. Every variation that sounds exciting on paper is another thing we have to make work before the deadline.

We’re excited, and we’re worried we won’t finish in time. Right now, we want to get the first sequence working and see whether playing it feels as good as imagining it did.
