# DevLog #4 — Figuring out where it all happens

We've been figuring out where everyone in Terminus spends their time. Having a cast makes it much easier to get interested in a place: we can start imagining who works there, who drops by, and what might bring us back after a quest.

The inn is a good example. Our current plan gives it a dining room and a small service yard. One quest idea has two people wanting to use that yard at the same time, one for training and the other for deliveries. It's still a proposal, but we like how an ordinary space can give people a reason to disagree. The yard has a job to do before the player ever arrives.

We've gathered the locations into 14 sheets, covering places like the guild halls, a workshop and the arena. Each sheet connects the place to the people and scenes that might use it. That has helped us picture the city more clearly, even though we haven't settled its geography yet.

For the visual novel, we're planning static backgrounds with separate character images on top. We're also thinking about where everyone can stand and what the dialogue box will cover. A detail we want players to notice needs to stay visible once the conversation starts.

The catalogue currently suggests 19 background views. Those are ideas for the art work ahead, not finished images or a final production count. Seeing them written down does make the deadline feel closer.

Still, we're excited to have places we can imagine returning to. We'd like a visit to the inn later in the game to feel familiar because of the conversations you've already had there. Getting that feeling into an actual scene is the next bit of work.
